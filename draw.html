    how to practice
    • Draw every line in one confident stroke, moving from your shoulder. Don't go back over it.
    • Draw through the box: add the back edges you can't see, so you can check its shape.
    • Edges that run the same way in 3D should all aim at the same vanishing point.
    • Turn on "vanishing lines", then extend your own edges in your head to check them.
    • Two edges are already on the pad (in gray) so your box starts in the right spot. Build the rest from them.
    • Press "check" to lay the correct box over yours. Each edge turns green, orange or red, and you get a score with tips.
    • On "auto", each of the day's 10 boxes gives you less help, and the perspective moves from 1-point to 2-point to 3-point.
    • Start with 1-point, then move on to 2-point and 3-point.
