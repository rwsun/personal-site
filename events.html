2025-2026,

I spent a year in the US as a developer & event organizer for Hack Club.

I ran online engineering education programs & in-person hackathons worldwide!

There were ups and downs - times I was missed Canada so much, where I spent weekends working, where I was stressed enough to walk into a (glass) door.

But I also met my best friends, traveled to countries I've never even dreamed of before, and grew exponentially as a developer & event organizer.

So... Work hard, put yourself out there, surround yourself with different types of people -

And take that leap of faith!
